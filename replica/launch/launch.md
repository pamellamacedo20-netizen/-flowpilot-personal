# Launch draft

## One-line positioning

Build conversational automations visually, monitor whether your channels are actually healthy, and know what your usage is costing before it surprises you.

## First ICP

Small service businesses and creator-led teams using Instagram and WhatsApp for lead capture, qualification and appointment handoff.

## Launch scope

Do not launch commercially until the production preflight passes. Initial supported channels should be Instagram and WhatsApp only; expand after provider reliability is measured.

## Pricing hypothesis — not final

Prefer simple included usage + explicit overage meter rather than ambiguous contact counting. Validate economics after real provider costs, support load and infrastructure costs are known.

## Required proof before marketing claims

- Successful webhook delivery rate.
- Outbound send success rate.
- Median automation execution latency.
- Channel reconnect recovery time.
- Lead conversion analytics correctness.
